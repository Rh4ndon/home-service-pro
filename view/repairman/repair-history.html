Repair Detail - Aug 12, 2026

Client: Corazon Aquino
Date: Aug 12, 2026
Appliance: Microwave Oven
Status: Completed
Duration: 2 hours
Ticket ID: #1035
Issue Reported:

Client reported that the microwave was not heating food. The turntable was rotating but no heat was produced. Display panel and buttons were functioning normally.

Diagnosis:

Faulty magnetron identified as the root cause. The high-voltage diode was also found to be defective. Capacitor tested within normal range.

Work Performed:

Replaced magnetron and high-voltage diode. Cleaned waveguide cover and interior. Tested heating at various power levels. All functions restored to normal operation.

Parts Used:

Magnetron (Samsung OM75P), High-Voltage Diode (2KV 350mA)
